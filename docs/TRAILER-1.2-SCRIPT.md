# BMX Cruisers 1.2 — trailer script

Duration: 75 seconds. Text-led, without narration. A 4K master and a 1080p sharing copy. Status: approved by the owner and produced on November 1, 2026. Final videos, thumbnail, upload description and verification reports are in `releases/1.2.0/media/`.

Published video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GMSA1Eg3nrI

All game footage was captured from the original C64 game running in an emulator and the current Windows executable. The opening transformation and flying logo are editorial effects. Feature shots show the actual game.

| 0:00–0:06 | Open on the original C64 title, then a short real ride toward the first bridge. Preserve the original 4:3 picture, framed against black. Small label identifies BMX Racers on Commodore 64. | REMEMBER THE RIDE? | Original C64 sound. Let the melody establish itself. |

A NEW VIEW. | The melody crosses into the raspy electric-guitar arrangement; percussion enters on the reveal. |

Return to the rider on the trail. | A CLASSIC CHALLENGE. / BUILT WITH UNREAL ENGINE 5. | The arrangement shifts into its heavily overdriven guitar section. Bring in water and park ambience briefly. |
| 1:00–1:08 | A clean bridge pass, the final approach, the checkerboard finish and vivid red, gold, green and white fireworks. Let the largest burst fill the frame. | BEAT THE COURSE. / LIGHT UP THE FINISH. | Finish cue, fireworks and a final guitar flourish. |
| 1:08–1:15 | The logo returns with a shorter impact and settles on a clean end card. Keep the download address visible for at least five seconds. Small attribution beneath it. | BMX CRUISERS / PLAY ON WINDOWS / RETROCOMPUTERIST.COM | Final chord sustains and fades naturally. |

End-card attribution: “Based on BMX Racers — David Darling & Richard Darling / Mastertronic. Unofficial recreation.”

The visual style follows the yellow outlined logo, charcoal panels and red accents already in the game. Captions stay short and avoid covering the rider or the next obstacle. The opening match transition will be built around a location verified in both captures.
